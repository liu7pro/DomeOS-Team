What We Do

Make the system lighter, not smaller

Slimming is not deleting features. It is spending every resource where it matters.

01

Slim

Remove redundant preinstalled apps and unnecessary background services to cut boot and idle load.

02

Keep

Core features, drivers and update capability stay complete. Daily use is not affected.

03

Maintain

Track releases and feedback over time, keeping every change small and stable.
